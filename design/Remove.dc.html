Why a key
The board is readable by anyone with the link, and this writes to the database. The key is typed once and kept in your browser — never compiled into the page, which would hand it to every visitor and gate nothing.
Why a flag, not a delete
Collection upserts on (source_id, external_id) and sets the row active every time it sees the posting. A deleted row simply comes back. The flag is a column the collector never writes, so it survives.
It also stops the email
Removed postings are excluded from the digest query too, so a role you have already struck off does not arrive in the next email three days later.
